Duebird

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Duebird is a subscription tracker for Android, published by Dreamcast Information Technology, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. It covers the Duebird app only.

The short version

  • There is no account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your subscription details (names, prices, dates, notes, payment labels) stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Duebird does not connect to your bank and does not read your email or text messages.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google uses your phone's advertising ID to do that.
  • If you leave "Anonymous stats" on, the app sends anonymous usage events and crash reports to Google Firebase. You can turn this off in Settings.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is saved in a database on your phone:

  • your subscriptions: service name, plan, price, currency, billing cycle, dates, status, notes, payment label (for example "Visa ••42"), reminders and payment history;
  • your settings, such as home currency, language, theme, payday and reminder time;
  • feedback you wrote that has not been sent yet.

Receipts and screenshots. When you paste a receipt, share one into Duebird or pick a picture, the text is read on your phone using Google's on-device text recognition. The picture and its text are not uploaded to us or to Google. Google's text recognition component may send Google technical performance data about itself.

Reminders. Reminders and price-change alerts are created on your phone. We do not run a server that sends you messages.

App lock. If you turn on App lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or PIN. Duebird only learns whether the check passed.

Widget and share cards. The home screen widget shows your next charges on your own phone. A share card is a picture made on your phone; it goes only where you choose to send it.

2. Backups

  • Google backup. Android's automatic backup may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account, so it comes back when you reinstall or change phones. Google runs this backup and you control it in your phone's backup settings. We cannot see it.
  • Backup file. "Export backup" creates a file with all your subscriptions and hands it to the app you choose (for example Drive, Files or email). The file is not password protected, so keep it somewhere safe. From then on it is covered by the service you saved it to.

3. What leaves your phone

Anonymous usage stats (Google Firebase Analytics)

When stats are on, the app sends events about how it is used, for example that a screen was opened, a subscription was added, a reminder was opened or the Pro page was viewed. An event can include:

  • the catalog ID of a well-known service you picked from our list (for example "netflix"), with its billing cycle and currency code;
  • counts and timings, such as how many subscriptions you track (as a range) and how long setup took;
  • the name of a setting you changed (never its value);
  • whether you have Pro, the Pro plan bought and its price and currency;
  • what an ad shown in the app earned;
  • a service name you typed, only when you tap "Suggest it" to ask us to add that service.

The app never sends names you typed for your own subscriptions, prices you pay, dates, notes or payment labels.

Firebase also receives technical data with these events: an app instance ID, your phone's advertising ID, phone model, Android version, language, app version and an approximate location (country or city level) worked out from your IP address. We see this as grouped statistics and use it to understand which features work and to fix problems. We keep analytics data for up to 14 months.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

When stats are on and the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent with the technical details of the error, phone model, Android version, app version, time and an installation ID. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Your choice about stats and crash reports

You can switch both off at any time in Settings > Anonymous stats, or on the first screen before you start. In regions where the law requires consent (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), stats and crash reports stay off unless you agree in Google's consent form.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, native, full-screen and rewarded video ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners collect your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device information and how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalised where the law allows it or where you have agreed.

  • Where required, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it in Settings > Ad privacy.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Privacy > Ads).
  • Duebird Pro removes all ads.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro so it can unlock it.

Invite friends

Your invite link contains an anonymous code made from a one-way hash of your phone's Android ID. When a friend installs from your link and adds their first subscription, their phone sends one record to our Firebase database: your code, a similar anonymous ID for their phone (so an install is counted once) and the time. Nothing else is sent, and the record does not include names or subscriptions. The app reads only the number of installs under your code. We keep these records while the invite programme runs.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your star rating, the reason you picked, the text you typed, the app version, Android version, phone language and country. It is not linked to your name or to your subscriptions, so please do not put personal details in the text. We keep feedback for as long as it helps us improve Duebird. If you email us instead, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Other connections the app makes

These requests carry no information about you or your subscriptions, but as with any internet request the server sees your IP address:

  • app settings from Google Firebase Remote Config (with a Firebase installation ID);
  • the catalog of services and prices, a file hosted on GitHub Pages;
  • daily exchange rates from public rate files (hosted on jsDelivr, Cloudflare Pages and open.er-api.com), only if you track a subscription in another currency;
  • Google Play, when you rate the app or buy Pro.

Cancel links and guides open the website or app of the service you want to cancel. Those services have their own privacy policies.

4. Who receives data

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers the app needs:

  • Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Firestore, Google Play): ads, statistics, crash reports, the invite counter, feedback storage and purchases. Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub and the exchange-rate hosts: file downloads only.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to a successor if the app is transferred to another company, under this policy.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the provider's certification under an approved data privacy framework.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show reminders and price-change alerts. Optional.
  • Fingerprint and face unlock: for App lock. Optional.
  • Internet: for the connections listed above.
  • Run at startup: so reminders still arrive after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: for reminders and touch feedback.
  • Advertising ID: for ads and statistics.

Duebird does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or call log. Pictures are read only when you pick or share them yourself.

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: data stays until you delete it. Remove a subscription in the app, or erase everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling Duebird.
  • Google backup: delete it from your Google account's backup settings.
  • Stats and crash reports: turn them off in Settings. Data already sent is deleted automatically after the periods given above.
  • Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Anything else: email us at the address below. Because there is no account, we usually cannot tell which anonymous records are yours, but we will help where we can.

7. Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox, and by App lock if you turn it on. Everything the app sends travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your rights

EEA, UK and Switzerland. Dreamcast Information Technology is the controller of the limited data described in section 3. We rely on your consent for statistics, crash reports and personalised ads; on our legitimate interests for non-personalised ads, the invite counter, feedback and keeping the app working and secure; and on performance of a contract for purchases. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings > Anonymous stats and Settings > Ad privacy). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

United States, including California. We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through AdMob may count as "sharing" or "selling" under some state laws; you can opt out in Settings > Ad privacy or by deleting your advertising ID in Android settings. Depending on your state you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights. The categories involved are identifiers (advertising ID, app and installation IDs), internet activity (app usage and ad interactions), approximate location from IP address and purchase information.

To use any of these rights, email us. We may need more information to find data related to you, because Duebird has no accounts.

9. Children

Duebird is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age where consent is required in your country. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it where we can.
